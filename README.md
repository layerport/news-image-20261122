Entry for the [news-image-20261122 bounty on make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261122): one 1024×1024 image summing up Sunday, 22 November 2026 in world news.

Lead story: tankers hit near Hormuz — Iran sends a convoy to break the US blockade, while Trump is said to favour economic pressure over a new strike. Also in the image: Houthi missiles at Riyadh intercepted; COP31 in Antalya in overtime, still no deal; the EU–Indonesia trade deal signed after ten years of talks; the Hartha power station fire in Basra kills 4 and injures 35; toxic alcohol kills 11 in Manipur with 180+ in hospital; a M7.2 quake in Maluku, no tsunami threat.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to the output directory as `entry.png`. Deterministic — every run produces the same bytes.
